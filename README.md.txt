# South Gujarat RMC Market Entry Feasibility Study

## Project Type
Business Analytics | Financial Modelling | Power BI | Market Research | Strategy Consulting Case Study

## Disclaimer
This project is an independent business analytics portfolio project prepared using publicly available data and consultant assumptions. It is not commissioned, endorsed, or affiliated with UltraTech Cement Limited.

## Project Overview
This project evaluates the feasibility of entering the Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) market in South Gujarat. The model combines market sizing, project-level demand estimation, competitor analysis, infrastructure pipeline assessment, financial modelling, risk analysis, dashboard reporting, and executive recommendation.

The objective was to build an end-to-end consulting-style feasibility model that supports investment decision-making for a phased RMC market-entry strategy.

## Business Problem
South Gujarat has a large and fragmented construction market across residential, commercial, mixed-use, plotted development, industrial, and infrastructure demand. The key business question was whether a new RMC market entry could be financially viable, operationally practical, and strategically attractive.

## Key Questions Answered
- What is the Total Addressable Market?
- Which districts contribute the highest reachable demand?
- What share of the market can realistically be captured?
- Which competitors create the highest pressure?
- What is the expected revenue, EBITDA, NPV, IRR, ROI, and payback?
- What risks could affect market entry?
- Should the project proceed under a phased rollout?

## Key Outputs
- Total projects analyzed: 3,087
- Total estimated demand: 44.2M m³
- TAM market value: ₹26,760 Cr
- Base case NPV: ₹30.8 Cr
- Base case IRR: 40.8%
- Base case payback: 2.9 years
- Recommendation: GO, with phased market entry
